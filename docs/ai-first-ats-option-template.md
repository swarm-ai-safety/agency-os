## Example (Filled): "AI-First ATS as Vertical Use Case"

### Opportunity Snapshot
- Option name: AI-first recruiting workflow on Agency-OS
- Decision owner: CPO
- Date: 2026-03-07
- Stage: Pilot

### User Problem
- Primary user: founder-led hiring teams and talent leads
- Current pain: high recruiter/referral economics with fragmented tooling and low automation leverage
- Why now: token economics can be cheaper than referral fees if quality remains high
- Existing workaround: manual sourcing + legacy ATS + agency recruiting

### Outcome Definition
- Target business outcome: prove lower cost-per-hire with equal or better quality
- User success metric: qualified slate in under 72 hours
- Company success metric: pilot customers reach target hires with positive ROI
- Time to measurable signal: 8-12 weeks

### Value vs. Cost Thesis
- Value created per successful hire: $10K-$20K benchmark (referral-equivalent economics)
- Token/automation budget per hire: up to $10K with strict governance and quality thresholds
- Gross margin target: >=60% at steady state
- Breakeven volume assumption: 2-3 hires per month per active customer

### Product Wedge
- Core workflow to win first: sourcing + ranking + outreach orchestration with human approval gates
- Why incumbents are weak: legacy ATS tools optimize workflow logging, not autonomous execution quality
- Defensible advantage: governance + agent orchestration + cost controls as infrastructure

### Scope (V1)
- In scope: candidate sourcing, scoring explainability, outreach sequencing, recruiter-in-the-loop approvals
- Out of scope: full ATS replacement, payroll/onboarding, enterprise integrations beyond 1-2 systems
- Dependencies: legal review for candidate data usage, deliverability stack, hiring manager feedback loop

### Go-To-Market Fit
- Buyer: startups and SMB talent leaders with urgent hiring needs
- Economic buyer: founder/COO/head of talent
- Distribution channel: founder network + targeted outbound
- Sales motion: pilot-first with ROI proof

### Risks and Mitigations
- Product risk: low precision in candidate ranking
- Model/quality risk: hallucinated candidate fit rationale
- Compliance/legal risk: outreach and personal data handling
- Adoption risk: recruiter distrust of autonomous actions
- Mitigations: mandatory approval gates, audit logs, confidence thresholds, staged autonomy

### Build / Partner / Defer Recommendation
- Recommendation: Defer as core product; pursue as vertical pilot after core launch
- Rationale:
  - Misaligned with current core roadmap focus
  - Strong strategic fit as downstream use case for Agency-OS infrastructure
  - Pilot can validate economics without committing to ATS platform scope
- Required commitments: 1 PM + 1 eng pod + legal review support for pilot window

### Decision Gate
- Gate criteria to continue: 3 pilot customers hit quality + ROI targets for 2 consecutive months
- Kill criteria: cannot reach quality threshold without excessive human intervention
- Next review date: 2026-05-01
